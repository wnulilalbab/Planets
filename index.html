🌍 Why Is There Night?
"Wait... where does the sun GO at night? Did it disappear forever? 😰"
👆 Click Earth to spin it!

The Sun doesn't move — Earth does! Earth spins like a top, once every 24 hours. When your side faces the Sun ☀️ it's DAY. When your side faces away 🌑 it's NIGHT!

⭐ What Are Stars?
"The sky is full of tiny lights! Are they fireflies? Or holes in the sky?! 🌌"
👆 Tap any star to learn about it!

Stars are giant balls of fire, just like our Sun! They look tiny because they are incredibly far away. The closest star (after our Sun) is 4.2 light-years away — its light takes 4 years to reach us! 🤯

But one "star" looks bigger and rounder... that's not a star at all! →
🌕 The Moon
"That glowing ball — why does it change shape every night?! Is it magic? 🔮"
🌕 Full Moon
← drag to change the phase →

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite — it orbits around us! It doesn't make its own light — it reflects sunlight like a mirror. As it orbits, we see different lit parts → that's why it changes shape over ~29 days! 🌑🌒🌓🌔🌕🌖🌗🌘

The Moon orbits Earth. Earth orbits the Sun... along with 7 other planets! →
